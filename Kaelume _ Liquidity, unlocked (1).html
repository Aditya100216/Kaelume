LAYER 01

Supply

Deposit HYPE, kHYPE, or USDC into one shared pool. This is the base layer, and it earns when others borrow.

LAYER 02

Collateral

Your supplied assets count as collateral. Each asset has its own limit, and kHYPE is treated more cautiously.

LAYER 03

Borrow

Borrow against that collateral without selling it. You can only borrow what the pool actually holds.

Safety

A live health gauge and a risk lab show what a price drop would do before it happens. Repay to safety in one tap.
